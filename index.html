Collin Haese

I’m a mechanical engineer and PhD researcher at The University of Texas at Austin. I use medical imaging, finite element modeling, and whole-heart experiments to study how repair devices change tricuspid valve mechanics.

Selected work

Simulating tricuspid valve repair

I build subject-specific finite element models from medical imaging and mechanical data. I’ve used them to study how annuloplasty device shape and size affect valve mechanics and how assumptions about annular motion influence edge-to-edge repair simulations.

Measuring repair mechanics in whole hearts

I designed and fabricated a mock flow loop, force transducers, and a handheld catheter system. These tools let us measure leaflet strain, annular forces, and flow during tricuspid valve repair in the lab.

Structural analysis of heavy-duty vehicles

At Oshkosh Corporation, I built finite element models to evaluate vehicle and component strength, durability, and safety. I worked with design engineers and presented findings and design recommendations to customers.
